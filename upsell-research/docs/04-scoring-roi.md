# Scoring & ROI engines

Pure functions in `packages/shared/src/engines/` — no I/O, no LLM, fully unit-tested with golden values. The LLM never runs this math.

## 1. Upsell score (100 points, brief §5)

| Area | Max | Full points when | Inputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Packages exist | 20 | ≥6 sellable packages found (linear: `min(20, found×3.33)` → round) | `packages(kind=found, included=false)` |
| Sellable online | 25 | packages in booking flow, or ≥1 package page with price + buy affordance; partial: price shown without buy = 12, buy affordance only = 8 | detected engine capability + package page scan |
| Pre-arrival reach | 20 | upsell tool detected (12) AND reaches beyond online check-in (8); manual-payment-link signal = 4 bonus within area, capped at 20 | `detected_tech` |
| Presentation | 15 | share of packages having photo AND price AND description: `15 × (full/total)` | `packages.has_*` |
| Personalization | 10 | rare; guest-specific offer signals (dynamic pricing, bid systems, per-segment landing). Most hotels score 0–3 | tech fingerprints + JS markers |
| Mobile | 10 | booking page passes 390px checks (overflow 4, tap targets 3, load ≤3s 3) | worker mobile-check; **skipped ⇒ area excluded, `score_out_of=90`** |

Grades: A ≥85 · B 70–84 · C 55–69 · D 40–54 · F <40 (grade computed on `scoreTotal/scoreOutOf×100`).
Every area emits `{area, points, max, skipped?, finding, fix}` — finding/fix one-liners generated by sonnet but constrained to the deterministic area facts (03 §4).

## 2. Missed revenue (with the capture step)

```
bookings      = rooms × occupancy × 365 / avgStayNights          // occ 0.65, stay 2.2 defaults
spend         = priceBand(otaNightlyRate, starRating)            // §3
potential     = bookings × takeRate × spend                      // takeRate 8%..15%
capture(score)= piecewise by grade, linear within band:           // labeled internal assumption
                F→10% · D→25% · C→40% · B→55% · A→70%
missed        = potential × (1 − capture)
display range = [potential(8%)·(1−c), potential(15%)·(1−c)]       // rounded to nearest $1k
```

Labeled on the report: "Estimate. Industry take-rate range applied to your scale; assumes capture typical for your grade (industry data, vendor reported inputs)."

### Worked example — The Dolli (validates against the field test)

46 rooms · 65% · 2.2 nights ⇒ **4,961 bookings/yr**. OTA $1,220 ⇒ band $450; 5★ bump ⇒ **$750**.

| Take rate | Potential | Capture (score 45 → D ≈ 30.4%) | **Missed** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8% | $297.7k | 69.6% | **$207k** |
| 11.5% (mid) | $427.7k | 69.6% | **$298k** |
| 15% | $558.1k | 69.6% | **$388k** |

With the unbumped $450 band: potential $178.6k–$334.9k ⇒ missed $124k–$233k. Compare the field test's flat-$95 version ($38k–71k as *potential*) — the banded + capture formula both fixes the luxury undercount (learning 1) and applies the brief's capture step the test skipped. All figures labeled estimates.

## 3. ROI & payback (brief §7)

Inputs (all prefilled from report, editable, live): rooms, occupancy, avgStay, currentUpsellRevenue/yr, buildPrice ($15,000 default).

```
projectedYearly  = bookings × takeRate(mid 11.5% by default; scenario low 8 / high 15) × spend
addedYearly      = projectedYearly − currentUpsellRevenue
paybackMonths    = buildPrice / (addedYearly / 12)                  // guard addedYearly ≤ 0 → "n/a"
costUpLayer3yr   = buildPrice + (carePlan ? 36 × 1000 : 0)          // $15k ⇒ $51k w/ care
costSaaS3yr_a    = 0.10 × projectedYearly × 3                       // commission model
costSaaS3yr_b    = 4 × rooms × 36                                   // per-room model
keptUpLayer3yr   = projectedYearly × 3 − costUpLayer3yr
keptSaaS3yr      = projectedYearly × 3 − costSaaS3yr_a|b
```

Low/middle/high scenarios shown side by side (take-rate 8/11.5/15%). Every benchmark cell carries its source label. Live-edit UX: client recomputes instantly via the same imported pure function; debounced (500ms) persist to `reports.inputs` + `roi_edited` event.
