# GuestSpace (formerly Drop.in)

Checked 1 October 2026. Site: https://guestspace.ai/

## 1. What it is

GuestSpace is an Amsterdam-based guest journey and front desk automation platform, rebranded in 2026 from Drop.in (idropin.com now serves the GuestSpace site). It sells a branded web app (no download) that bundles agentic email and WhatsApp pre-arrival campaigns, online check-in with payments, automated upsells and room upgrades, digital keys and a check-in kiosk. Its pitch is "staffless guest journeys" and guest activation, not upselling first. Buyers are hotels, hostels, serviced apartments and aparthotels, mostly independent and small-group operators (customers named on site include The Alfred Hotel, Kabannas Group, Wotels, City Trip Hostels, Thistle Suites, Your Apartment, Il Momento Bern, Hotel the Exchange, Hotel Juliani, Anne & Max Utrecht). It claims "Trusted by 100+ hospitality venues around the world" (vendor reported). Country focus is Europe: Hotel Tech Report reviewers are from the Netherlands (11), United Kingdom (8), Portugal (5), Switzerland (2), Norway, Malta and Belgium (1 each) (counted from Hotel Tech Report profile). Site footer says "Made in the Netherlands, Amsterdam" and "Made for Europe". Founded late 2021 by Derek de Jonge van Zwijnsbergen and Tjibbe Beckers, pre-seed from 3XA, ASIF Ventures and GraduateEntrepreneur (per search result snippets of a 2022 funding post, original page unreachable).

## 8. What UpLayer should copy, and where UpLayer can beat them

Copy:

| Idea | Why |
|---|---|
| Lead with an activation number ("X% of guests open and act") before revenue | Upsell revenue is capped by how many guests see the offer. GuestSpace makes this the headline |
| Email plus WhatsApp as default channels | Their reviewers credit WhatsApp with higher engagement than previous platforms |
| PMS webhook on booking creation triggers the first message with a unique guest URL | Clean, proven trigger pattern (Mews workflow) |
| Live-availability gating on room upgrades and early check-in | Prevents selling inventory that does not exist |
| Write purchased add-ons back to the PMS folio | Hotels expect upsells to land in the PMS bill |
| Done-for-you onboarding in two sessions | Removes setup friction for small hotels |
| Many named testimonials with role and property | Strong, specific social proof format |

Beat them:

| Opportunity | How UpLayer wins |
|---|---|
| Upsell-first product | Make the upsell landing page the product, not a tab inside a check-in app |
| Real per-guest personalization | Show the guest which offer was chosen and why (party size, stay length, booking channel, lead time, purpose, past stays), with generated copy and bundle per guest. Publish the logic |
| Payment choice | Pay upfront at a discount vs reserve now and pay at arrival. GuestSpace shows neither |
| Clear merchant of record | Platform-processed payments with payouts, or hotel-as-merchant options, stated plainly |
| Source-agnostic ingestion | Accept bookings from PMS, channel manager or portal, not only Mews, Apaleo, Stayntouch |
| Upsell analytics | Offer-level conversion, revenue per arrival, uplift vs control. Their reporting is a known weak spot |
| Revenue proof | Publish upsell revenue metrics and a guarantee tied to upsell revenue, which GuestSpace does not |
| Transparent pricing | State the unit and allow a trial or pilot without a sales call |
| Site quality | A polished, error-free landing page is itself a differentiator against them |
