# SiteMinder, Guest Engagement add-on (formerly GuestJoy)

Status: competitor research for the AI-personalized hotel upsell platform. Checked 1 October 2026.

## 1. What it is

SiteMinder is a hotel commerce platform best known for its channel manager. It also sells a booking engine, website builder, SiteMinder Pay (powered by Stripe), metasearch (Demand Plus), GDS, Business Intelligence, Dynamic Revenue Plus and Multi-Property. It is not a full PMS for most customers: it connects to 350+ PMSs and RMSs. Its sister brand Little Hotelier is the PMS for small properties. Upselling sits in the **Guest Engagement** add-on, which came from SiteMinder's acquisition of GuestJoy (Estonia), announced 23 August 2022. GuestJoy had been a SiteMinder partner since 2017.

- Customers: independents (SiteMinder and SiteMinder Plus plans, priced by room count) up to groups and chains (custom quote).
- Customer count: "56,000+ hotels globally", 150+ countries, 3,000+ partners, 140m+ annual reservations, AU$85 billion hotel revenue generated annually (About page, vendor reported). The Little Hotelier booking engine page says "53,000+ hoteliers", so first-party figures conflict.
- Guest Engagement upselling specifically: Hotel Tech Report lists about 2,834 hotels (third-party reported, not verified).

## 2. Built-in upsell features

**Channels**
- Email (pre-stay to post-stay), plus a unified inbox for email, SMS, WhatsApp, Booking.com and Airbnb messages.
- App-free guest access (no download) to online check-in, a digital directory reached by QR code and surveys.
- Booking engine add-ons at the point of booking. Mews' 2026 blog describes SiteMinder upsells as surfacing "through the booking engine at the point of booking".
- FAQ: emails reach Booking.com guests even when Booking.com masks the address.

**Timing**
- "Schedule Automated Sends" for every stage. FAQ: send times are fully customizable "before arrival, at check-in, during the stay, and after departure".
- Offers "prior-to and during their stay".

**Personalization and AI**
- "Personalised Offers ... custom-made to suit your guests interests, travel situation, and type of stay". Over 20 languages.
- Mechanism (rules, segments or AI): not documented on first-party pages checked.
- Hotel Tech Report lists guest segmentation, dynamic upgrade pricing and A/B testing (third-party listing, not confirmed on SiteMinder pages). No AI offer generation documented by SiteMinder.

**Payment options**
- Not documented on the Guest Engagement page.
- Third-party listings say guests pay through a purchase link (Stripe and wallets mentioned) and charges can post to the guest folio (third-party reported).
- SiteMinder Pay handles deposits, payments and refunds across channels.
- Upfront discount versus pay-at-arrival full price: not found.

**Admin config**
- "Offer an unlimited range of add-ons and offerings". "Easy subscription" tick boxes for mailing lists. "Designs and functionality custom-built for hoteliers".
- Hotel Tech Report mentions a content management system for products, services and room upgrades, and an "Upsell Fulfillment Tracking" dashboard (third-party reported).

**Analytics**
- "Rich Reporting ... simple yet thorough performance reports" (vendor page). Upsell-specific metrics: not found on first-party pages.

## 3. Is it an add-on, and what does it cost

From https://www.siteminder.com/pricing/, rendered in a browser on 1 October 2026 with geolocated USD prices:
- **SiteMinder:** from **$135 USD per month+**. Includes channel manager, Channels Plus (transaction fees may apply), PMS integration, performance and pace insights, 24/7 support and payment processing.
- **SiteMinder Plus:** from **$179 USD per month+**. Adds booking engine, website builder, competitor rates, rate parity, direct booking plug-in apps and Demand Plus.
- **Groups and Chains:** contact sales.
- Footnote: "Pricing is based on room count. This price is for an independent property with 5 rooms." 14-day free trial, cancel any time.
- **Guest Engagement is an optional add-on** on both independent plans, with an "Enquire" button and **no published price**.
- Third-party estimates (estimate, not SiteMinder published):
  - GuestJoy pricing "from EUR 150 per month" (softwarefinder.com).
  - SiteMinder Plus at $109 to $129 per month on review sites, which conflicts with the live $179.

## 6. Limits

- **Tied to SiteMinder data.** Guest Engagement works from reservations that flow through SiteMinder (channel manager, booking engine, connected PMS). Hotel Tech Report reviews mention "mapping errors affecting booking integration" (third-party reported).
- **Cross-PMS is partial.** SiteMinder connects to many PMSs, so it is less PMS-locked than a PMS-native tool. But the hotel must buy SiteMinder's platform first, and Guest Engagement cannot be bought on its own.
- **Generic offers.** "Personalised offers" are claimed without any documented mechanism. No AI offer generation, per-guest landing pages or dynamic pricing on first-party pages.
- **Weak payment options.**
  - Upsell payment flow not documented by SiteMinder.
  - No documented prepay discount versus pay-at-arrival price.
  - SiteMinder Pay is a separate enquiry product with transaction fees.
- **No public price and no proof.** Opaque cost and zero upsell results on the page make an ROI case hard for the hotelier.
- **Upsell is a side feature** inside a communications suite, not the core of the product.

## 7. API and integration surface

- **Docs:** https://developer.siteminder.com (GitBook, with `llms.txt` and Markdown versions). Five APIs:
  - **pmsXchange:** for PMSs and RMSs, SOAP plus REST. Two-way sync of availability, rates, restrictions and reservations, plus a payment transaction record.
  - **SiteConnect:** for booking channels.
  - **Channels Plus:** REST, for broad property access.
  - **SMX (SiteMinder Exchange):** for applications.
  - **Direct Booking:** REST, for hotels building their own engines.
- **Read reservations:** yes, through **SMX**. The "Application" integration type names "upselling engines" as a use case.
  - Reservations are pushed as OTA-standard SOAP XML. JSON is planned, not available.
  - Updates arrive as full re-sends without a diff.
  - Failed deliveries retry every 30 seconds, 3 attempts maximum.
  - Certify once and connect to all SMX data publishers. Hotels on an SMX-certified PMS can connect "without the need to be a SiteMinder customer".
  - Availability and rate data is not available from SiteMinder Channel Manager or Little Hotelier publishers.
- **Write charges back:** **No.** SMX FAQ: "Can I send data back to the publisher? Not yet. However, this is planned for a future enhancement." An upsell app on SMX cannot post a folio item or charge through SiteMinder. It would need a direct PMS API for write-back.
- **Webhooks:** SMX itself works as push delivery of reservation messages. No other webhook product found.
- **Partner terms:**
  - Apply at https://www.siteminder.com/integrations/apply-now/, then build, certify and go live.
  - Listing is in the SiteMinder Hotel App Store.
  - Commercial questions go to the Ecosystem team through Partner Contacts.
  - Partner fees: not found for SMX. Channels Plus has a separate commercial and commission section.

## 8. What UpLayer should copy, and where UpLayer can beat them

**Copy**
- Reach OTA guests through masked Booking.com addresses and unified OTA messaging. UpLayer needs the same reach or its offers miss most bookings.
- App-free guest access by link and QR code, and 20+ languages.
- Bundling upsell with online check-in and the digital directory. Hotels like one guest link.
- The room-count pricing model with a free trial, which makes buying easy for independents.

**Beat**
- **Proof:** SiteMinder's page has zero upsell numbers. UpLayer should lead with a measurable promise (for example, attachment rate and upsell revenue per booking against the hotel's own baseline) and a free upsell audit.
- **AI-personalized landing page per guest**, versus an undocumented "personalised offers" claim.
- **Two-price payment** (discounted prepay or full price at arrival) handled on UpLayer's own checkout, versus an undocumented payment flow.
- **Standalone purchase:** UpLayer can be bought without a channel manager subscription. Guest Engagement requires a SiteMinder plan.
- **Integration plan:** use SMX to read reservations from many PMSs in one certification, then write charges back through each PMS's own API (Mews Add order, Cloudbeds postItem), because SMX is read-only. Without a PMS write path, UpLayer collects payment itself and gives the hotel a reconciliation export.
