# Little Hotelier, built-in upsell (booking engine extras plus Guest Engagement add-on)

Status: competitor research for the AI-personalized hotel upsell platform. Checked 1 October 2026.

## 1. What it is

Little Hotelier is SiteMinder's all-in-one platform for small accommodation providers: B&Bs, guesthouses, boutique hotels, small hotels and inns. One subscription includes the PMS (calendar, front desk), channel manager, booking engine, mobile app and payments. Upselling comes from two places:
1. **Booking engine extras**, packages and promo codes, sold at the time of booking.
2. The **Guest Engagement** add-on, the same GuestJoy-derived product SiteMinder sells, for automated upsell emails, check-in forms, a digital directory and reviews.

- Customers: small properties. Third-party reviewers describe it as built for properties typically under 20 rooms (third-party reported). The pricing calculator defaults to 5 bookable rooms.
- Customer count: no Little Hotelier-specific count found. Pages say "Every hour a small property chooses Little Hotelier", 140+ countries, and "Powered by SiteMinder ... trusted by 53,000+ hoteliers worldwide" (vendor reported, SiteMinder-wide figure).

## 2. Built-in upsell features

**Channels**
- Booking engine: "add extras like breakfast or bike rentals, right from your website", plus promo codes and special discounts. 20+ languages and 75 currencies.
- Guest Engagement: one inbox for email, SMS, WhatsApp, Airbnb and Booking.com. "Tailored upsell emails or warm welcome texts". A digital directory with "upsell offers" by link or QR code.

**Timing**
- At booking (engine extras).
- Pre-arrival scheduled messages. The blog recommends an automated email or SMS 3-5 days before arrival.
- In-stay through the digital directory.

**Personalization and AI**
- Blog claim: Guest Engagement delivers "tailored upgrade and add-on offers automatically, segmented by traveller type".
- The Guest Engagement product page itself only says "tailored upsell emails". No segmentation or offer logic is documented in a help article found.
- On AI, the blog says: "For the moment, AI personalisation is far from a must for" small hotels, and "for small hotels with limited inventory, non-AI upsell strategies can be every bit as effective." No AI upsell feature exists.

**Payment options**
- Little Hotelier Payments (Stripe-based per third-party sources): no setup or subscription cost, transaction fees apply.
- Booking engine extras are paid with the booking.
- For Guest Engagement upsells, the blog's own sample email asks guests to "simply reply to this email or update your reservation online". No documented in-email checkout.
- Prepay discount versus pay-at-arrival price: not found. Promo codes and discounts apply to bookings, not to an upsell payment-timing choice.

**Admin config**
- Booking engine: extras, packages and promo codes, with look and feel matched to the property.
- Guest Engagement: scheduled messages, check-in forms that sync to the reservation record, a digital directory, and feedback surveys that route positive reviews to review sites.
- Detailed upsell product configuration docs: not found.

**Analytics**
- The blog recommends tracking upsell capture rate, average upsell value and RevPAG or RevPAR lift. It notes "most small properties don't monitor these data points".
- Upsell-specific reporting in the product: not found.

## 3. Is it an add-on, and what does it cost

From https://www.littlehotelier.com/pricing/, rendered in a browser on 1 October 2026:
- **Pro: from $179 USD per month** (excluding tax) at the default 5 bookable rooms.
  - Includes channel manager, booking engine, calendar, mobile app, competitor rate intelligence and rate parity insights.
  - Hotel Metasearch and Channels Plus carry variable booking fees.
  - Pricing "mostly depends on the number of rooms". Month to month, or annual with a discount. 30-day free trial.
- **Guest Engagement is a paid add-on** ("Add-on" on the feature table, listed under "Available add-ons"). **No price published.**
- Other add-ons: Hotel Metasearch, Channels Plus, Dynamic Revenue Plus (free for 3 months), Website Builder.
- Payments: "no set up or subscription costs ... however transaction fees apply".
- Third-party estimate for the add-on: not found.

## 6. Limits

- **Only works for Little Hotelier bookings.** Extras live in the Little Hotelier booking engine. Guest Engagement runs on Little Hotelier reservations (OTA bookings arrive through its channel manager).
- **No cross-PMS support.** A small group with properties on different systems cannot use it as one upsell layer.
- **Generic offers.** A static extras list in the booking engine, plus scheduled emails. No dynamic pricing, no per-guest offer page, and AI personalization explicitly dismissed.
- **Weak payment options.**
  - The sample pre-arrival email asks the guest to reply or update the reservation, which is not a one-click paid checkout.
  - No prepay discount versus pay-at-arrival option.
  - Payments are tied to Little Hotelier Payments, with transaction fees.
- **Opaque add-on cost.** Guest Engagement has no published price on top of $179 a month.

## 7. API and integration surface

- **No public Little Hotelier API.** Third-party reviewers report no customer-facing API (third-party reported: supergood.ai "API report card" grade F, hoteltechinsight.com). Integrations go through the SiteMinder Hotel App Store partner program, with a manual application, contract and certification.
- **Read reservations:** through **SiteMinder SMX**, where Little Hotelier is a data publisher. SMX FAQ: availability and rate data cannot be read from Little Hotelier ("Little Hotelier and The Channel Manager are not pushing ARI data to SMX"). Reservation data is available. Format is SOAP XML OTA messages, full re-send on each change, 3 retries at 30-second intervals.
- **Write charges back:** **No.** SMX FAQ: "Can I send data back to the publisher? Not yet." No other documented route to post a folio item or charge into Little Hotelier was found.
- **Webhooks:** none beyond SMX push delivery.
- **Partner terms:** apply through SiteMinder (https://www.siteminder.com/integrations/apply-now/). Commercial terms come from the SiteMinder Ecosystem team. Fees: not found.
- **Implication for UpLayer:** for Little Hotelier hotels, UpLayer can read bookings through SMX but must collect payment itself and give the hotel a reconciliation report or staff task, because it cannot write to the folio.

## 8. What UpLayer should copy, and where UpLayer can beat them

**Copy**
- The small-hotel language and the scripts. Hoteliers in this segment respond to "make sales in your sleep", ready-made pre-arrival email copy and simple metrics (capture rate, average upsell value).
- Self-serve buying: room-count pricing, a 30-day free trial, month to month. UpLayer's offer to this segment should be self-serve or done-for-you with no contract.
- Packages priced below the sum of their parts. UpLayer's AI can generate these bundles automatically.

**Beat**
- **Answer "small hotels don't need a dedicated upselling tool"** with a done-for-you setup and a guarantee tied to measured upsell revenue. The incumbent sells the tool, not the outcome.
- **One-click paid offer page** with prepay-at-a-discount versus pay-at-arrival, versus "reply to this email".
- **AI personalization** that Little Hotelier openly dismisses. UpLayer's position: it costs the hotelier no extra work because UpLayer writes the offers.
- **Works across systems** for owners with more than one property on different PMSs.
- **Integration reality:** read via SMX, collect payment on UpLayer, and send the hotel a daily "what to post and deliver" list. Be honest that folio write-back is not available for Little Hotelier.
